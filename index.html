2025 · Statistical / Applied ML

Personalized Antibiotic Therapy

A two-stage ML pipeline on 80,000 UTI patient records. Stage 1 predicts per-patient antibiotic resistance (Logistic Regression, kNN, Random Forest, GBT) with LASSO / Stepwise-AIC feature selection reducing 790 variables to 396 predictors. Stage 2 selects the lowest-resistance antibiotic via patient-similarity modeling and Youden's-J dynamic thresholds.
